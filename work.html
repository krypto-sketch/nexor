Work we have shipped

Two restaurant websites, live today. Here is what each one needed and what we built.

Firangii

Vegetarian restaurant, Kanpur · Design and development

The need

A way for customers to order straight from the restaurant instead of going through delivery apps that take a commission on every order.

What we built

  • A menu and order form where customers pick dishes and send the order on WhatsApp.
  • A mobile-first layout, since most customers browse on phones.
  • Search-ready page titles and descriptions.
Visit firangii.netlify.app →

The Silver Spoon

Restaurant and banquet, Sanai Lehari · Design and development

The need

An online home for a restaurant and banquet venue: show the space, the menu and the location, and make it easy to book a table.

What we built

  • A table-booking form (date, seating time, phone) that sends the reservation to the restaurant's WhatsApp.
  • Menu highlights with prices, a full-menu view and an order option.
  • Photo sections for the dining hall, lawn and entrance, with opening hours and location up front.
  • Link-preview and search setup, including structured restaurant data.
Visit thesilverspoon.vercel.app →
