How a project runs

Simple and in the open, so you always know what happens next.

  1. BriefA short call about your goals, customers and what the site must do. We then write up the scope and a quote.
  2. DesignMockups in Figma. You review them and we revise until you are happy.
  3. BuildWe code the approved design and test it on real phones.
  4. LaunchWe deploy the site and hand over the code and files.

Common questions

Do I own the code and designs?

Yes. When the project is done you get the code and the Figma files.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of pages and the features. We agree the scope and price in writing before we start.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size. We give you a timeline in the quote, after the first call.

What do you need from me?

Your logo and brand colors if you have them, your text and photos, and quick feedback on the designs. If you do not have photos or text yet, tell us and we will work out a plan.

What about changes after launch?

Tell us what you expect on the first call and we will put revisions and support in the quote.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you need. We reply with questions and a clear written quote before any work starts.
